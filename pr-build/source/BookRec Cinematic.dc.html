Why it matters

Collaborative filtering scales. Language makes it personal.

Grounded, never invented
The LLM only ever re-ranks the collaborative-filtering candidates on your shelf. Every stage re-validates ids against the shortlist — no hallucinated titles, ever.

Open any book and a neutral line tells you what it is — and one more on why it earns its rank for you. The full reasoning trace is one tap away.

No API key? The whole pipeline runs on a transparent, deterministic heuristic. Add a key and the same shelf upgrades to live reasoning.

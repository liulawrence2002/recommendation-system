## Sources & notes
- `exec_*` / `appendix_*` — generated by `bookrec/reports/run_eda.py`.
- `model_comparison.png` — generated by `make_model_comparison.py` (project root) from the
  **Step 5 held-out** numbers in the `erika model/` notebooks (90/10 split, seed 6604;
  k tuned by CV on train only).
- Do **not** use the Step 3 / cells 15-18-30-31 numbers from those notebooks
  (UBCF P@10 0.7479, R@10 0.8948): they fit on the full dataset incl. the test rows,
  so they are inflated by leakage. The held-out numbers in `model_comparison.png` are the
  reportable ones.
